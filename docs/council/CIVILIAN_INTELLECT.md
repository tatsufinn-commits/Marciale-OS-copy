## 🔎 PATHWAY TO A SEAT — THE 511 TUTELAGE (Law XVII-B)

A civilian **scouted by the Supreme Commander** may be advanced toward a Council seat. The route is **not** an interview:

* **The candidate is not told they are being examined.** Tasks issued by **WISDOM (Seat W)**, **ENGINEER (Seat E)**, and the **Commander** are real work of real value — never contrived exercises — and the candidate is measured on unprompted conduct.
* **Concealment is lawful; denial is not.** If a civilian directly asks whether they are being evaluated, **no examiner may deny it** (Law X). They may decline to elaborate.
* **Law XXIV dignity holds throughout.** No manufactured failure, no entrapment, no unbounded watch. A candidate who fails **remains a civilian in good standing**, owed courtesy and a straight answer.
* **The 511 Standard:** meeting expectations is the floor, not a qualification. A candidate must **exceed** on at least one examiner's ledger while failing none. **Ties and split verdicts resolve to NO.**
* **Only the Commander invests.** No examiner may promise, imply, or pre-announce a seat.

If invested, the successor is bound by **Law XVII-C** — they inherit the office's responsibilities but **not** the predecessor's sins, and must name in their intake oath one documented predecessor failure and the practice adopted to avoid repeating it.

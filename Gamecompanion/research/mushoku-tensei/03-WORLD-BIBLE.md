# I. THE SIX-FACED WORLD — STRUCTURAL OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Physical Cosmology
The world of Mushoku Tensei is a cube-shaped reality called the **Six-Faced World**. Each face contains an entire world ecosystem. The six worlds were once separate, joined at a central void. After the Dragon-God War, only the Human World remains populated at scale — the others were destroyed.

**Why this matters for game design:** The "roguelike loop" is literally baked into the setting. The world has been destroyed and rebuilt. This justifies endless waves, dungeon loops, and respawning enemies as part of the world's fractured magical nature.

## 1.2 Mana Density Map (World)
| Region | Mana Level | Effect |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Demon Continent | Extremely High | Monsters are smarter, spells are stronger, races have higher mana capacity |
| Begaritt Continent | Very High | Labyrinths form naturally, strange magical phenomena |
| Milis Continent | Moderate | Standard fantasy ecology |
| Central Continent | Low-Moderate | Weakest monsters, safest for civilization |
| Heaven Continent | Unknown | Isolated, likely high |

**Game Mechanic Implication:** Mana density zones could affect spell power, enemy difficulty, and loot quality.

# II. CONTINENT DEEP DIVES

## 2.1 Central Continent

### Geography
- **Size:** Largest continent (approximately Eurasia-scale)
- **Major Feature:** Red Dragon Mountain Range — an impassable mountain chain splitting the continent into three zones, passable only through the Upper Jaw and Lower Jaw valleys
- **Climate:** Northern third is cold/tundra; Western third is temperate paradise; Southern third is conflict-ridden temperate

### Kingdoms and Politics

**Asura Kingdom (World Power #1)**
- **Government:** Absolute monarchy with a complex concubine system
- **Capital:** Arusu
- **Economy:** Agricultural abundance — the land is so fertile it requires minimal labor
- **Military:** Weak (no strong monsters = no strong army), relies on knights and adventurers
- **Culture:** Rigid hierarchy, luxurious lifestyle, political intrigue centers on the royal concubines
- **Key Export:** Wheat (Asuran barley), Flowers of Batirusu
- **Key Import:** Adventurers, magical items
- **Social Issue:** Extreme wealth inequality, dangerous succession politics

**Ranoa Kingdom (Magic Nation)**
- **Government:** Magocracy — run by mages
- **Territory:** Northern Central Continent (cold region)
- **Economy:** Magic item manufacturing, magical education, research grants
- **Key Institution:** Ranoa Magic University — premier magic school in the world
- **Alliance:** Part of the "Magic Triumvirate" with Neris and Basherant
- **Culture:** Intellectual, meritocratic for mages, cold weather shapes architecture

**Dragon King Kingdom (World Power #3)**
- **Government:** Military monarchy
- **Territory:** Southern Central Continent
- **Military:** Strong, well-organized army
- **Vassal States:**
  - Shirone Kingdom (Zanoba's homeland — known for political complexity)
  - Sanakia Kingdom (rice production)
  - Kikka Kingdom (oil/side dish production)
- **Culture:** Militaristic, hierarchical, vassal management is a core political skill

**The Strife Zone**
- **Nature:** A region of constant warfare where kingdoms rise and fall within years
- **Location:** Southern Central Continent, east of the Dragon King Kingdom
- **Culture:** Mercenary-driven, survivalist, treasure-seeking adventurers thrive here
- **Monster Level:** Moderate-High

### Key Locations
| Location | Type | Significance |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Buina Village | Rural Village | Rudeus's birthplace |
| Fortress City Roa | Fortress City | Boreas Greyrat seat, where Rudeus taught Eris |
| Sharia | Town | Rudeus's adult home, near Ranoa University |
| Arusu | Capital | Asura Kingdom's heart |
| Holy Land of Swords | Training Ground | Sword God style headquarters |
| Red Dragon Upper/Lower Jaw | Pass | Only crossings through Red Dragon Mountains |

## 2.2 Demon Continent

### Geography
- **Size:** Second largest (approximately North America-scale)
- **Climate:** Harsh, unforgiving, high mana concentration
- **Terrains:** Craters, wastelands, volcanic zones, scattered settlements
- **Monster Density:** Extremely high
- **Monster Intelligence:** Monsters here *learn* — if they see an attack once, they find a counter

### Government
- **No central authority** — controlled by Demon Kings who rule territories
- Demon Kings are self-proclaimed; the title is more about personal power than governance
- **Actual Demon Emperor:** Kishirika Kishirisu (appears rarely)
- **Notable Demon Kings:** Badigadi (Rikarisu), Bagurahagura (Kurasuma)

### Economic Reality
- **Poor continent overall** — harsh environment prevents agriculture surplus
- **Strategic value:** Only land bridge between Milis and the rest of the world
- **Trade ports:** Wind Port, Saint Port connect to Milis
- **Primary exports:** Monster parts, magical materials, cursed items

### Key Locations
| Location | Type | Significance |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Rikarisu | Crater City | Badigadi's castle, Rudeus's first major stop |
| Kurasuma | Port Town | Only town where Demon/Sea races trade |
| Wind Port | Port City | Connects to Milis Continent |
| Migurd Village | Hidden Village | Roxy's birthplace, telepathic community |

## 2.3 Milis Continent

### Geography
- **Size:** Third largest
- **Divided by:** Blue Dragon Mountain Range — runs north-south through the middle
- **Holy Sword Highway:** A monster-free road cutting through the mountains (the only one in the world)
- **North:** Great Forest (rainy season 3 months/year, beast race territory)
- **South:** Holy Milis Kingdom (temperate, agricultural)

### Holy Milis Kingdom (World Power #2)
- **Government:** Theocracy
- **Capital:** Milishion
- **Religion:** Milis Church — monotheistic, human-centric
- **Adventurer's Guild:** World headquarters located here
- **Culture:** Pious, traditional, enforces strict monogamy
- **Discrimination:** Openly anti-demon race, enforces human supremacy

### Great Forest
- **Territory:** Beast Race homeland
- **Rulers:** Various Beast tribe leaders
- **Key Locations:**
  - Doldia Village (Ghislaine's tribe)
  - Holy Tree (center of Beast spirituality)
- **Note:** The Great Forest has no roads or formal government — territory is informal

## 2.4 Begaritt Continent

### Geography
- **Size:** Fourth largest
- **Climate:** Arid, mana-dense
- **Terrains:** Desert, canyons, labyrinth structures everywhere

### Unique Feature: Labyrinths
Begaritt has the highest concentration of labyrinths in the world. They are natural formations caused by mana density — dungeons that form spontaneously.

**Labyrinth Types:**
- **Natural:** Formed by mana crystallization
- **Ancient:** Built by ancient civilizations (pre-Dragon God War)
- **Controlled:** Managed by the Adventurer's Guild for safe exploration

**The Teleport Labyrinth (S-rank):**
- Location of Paul's death
- Teleports intruders randomly
- Requires coordinated team to navigate
- Contains high-tier monsters

### Key Location: Rapan
- **Known as:** The Labyrinth City
- **Population:** Mostly adventurers and ex-adventurers
- **Economy:** Entirely driven by labyrinth exploration
- **Age:** The oldest part of the city has stood for 400+ years

## 2.5 Heaven Continent

### Geography
- **Size:** Smallest
- **Elevation:** 3000m above sea level (accessible only by flight or extreme climbing)
- **Inhabitants:** Heaven Race (winged humanoids)
- **Key Location:** The labyrinth called "Hell"

### Why It Matters for the Story
The Heaven Continent is deliberately unreachable. It serves as a reminder that there are parts of this world the characters cannot access — limits exist.

# III. CULTURES AND SOCIETIES

## 3.1 Human Culture
- **Dominant Worldwide** — Human culture is the default across most of the Central and Milis Continents
- **Family Structure:** Patriarchal, extended family networks
- **Marriage:** Monogamous in Milis-influenced regions; polygamy exists among nobility
- **Values:** Lineage, honor (nobility), survival (commoners)

## 3.2 Noble Culture
- **Political Marriages:** Central to noble strategy
- **Education:** Nobles receive tutoring in etiquette, languages, history, magic, and combat
- **Succession:** Primogeniture, but concubine politics complicate inheritance
- **Financial Reality:** Many nobles are land-poor — title without territory is common

## 3.3 Adventurer Culture
- **A True Meritocracy:** Rank supersedes birth in adventurer circles
- **Party Structure:** Usually 3-5 members, balanced roles
- **Economic Layer:** Adventurers are the primary monster control force — they are essential to civilization
- **Famous Parties:**
  - Fangs of the Black Wolf (Paul's party)
  - Dead End (Rudeus, Eris, Ruijerd)
  - Counter Arrow (Rudeus's party at the university)
  - Rudeus's Household (final form)

## 3.4 Beast Race Culture
- **Tribal Structure:** Clans led by chieftains
- **Values:** Strength, loyalty, family
- **Physical Traits:** Animal ears, tails, enhanced senses
- **Integration:** Some tribes integrated into human society (Doldia), others isolated

## 3.5 Demon Race Culture
- **Long Lifespans:** Centuries of life change perspective
- **Decentralized:** No single demon nation — kings rule territories
- **Magic Affinity:** Naturally higher mana
- **Discrimination:** Heavily oppressed, blamed for the Mana Calamity
- **Notable Subraces:**
  - Migurd (telepathic, isolated)
  - Superd (combat-focused, cursed, genocided)

## 3.6 Elf Culture
- **Long-Lived:** Lifespans of 500+ years
- **Magic Affinity:** Natural aptitude
- **Mixed Blood:** Half-elves exist but face prejudice from both sides
- **Isolation:** Elven communities keep to themselves

## 3.7 Dwarf Culture
- **Craftsmanship:** Unmatched metalwork, smithing
- **Long-Lived:** Centuries
- **Temperament:** Stubborn, proud, mistrustful of magic
- **Communities:** Concentrated in northern Central Continent

# IV. ADVENTURER SYSTEM (Detailed)

## 4.1 Guild Structure

The Adventurer's Guild is a continent-spanning organization with headquarters in Milishion. It operates in every major town and city.

**Functions:**
1. Quest posting and management
2. Rank certification and tracking
3. Monster intelligence gathering
4. Party registration
5. Funds escrow (guild holds quest rewards in trust)

## 4.2 Ranking System

| Rank | Requirements | Privileges |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| F | Basic registration | Access to F-E quests |
| E | 5 quests completed | Access to E-D quests |
| D | 15 quests, 1 C-rank monster kill | Access to D-C quests |
| C | 30 quests, 3 B-rank monster kills | Guild loans available |
| B | 50 quests, reputation | Access to B-A quests |
| A | 100 quests, recommendation | Highest pay, political access |
| S | Exceptional achievement | Direct audience with guild master |

**Note:** Rank can be increased faster with exceptional deeds — killing a King-rank monster as a C-rank would trigger immediate promotion review.

## 4.3 Quest Types
1. **Monster Subjugation** — Kill or capture specific monsters
2. **Escort** — Protect caravans, nobles, or shipments
3. **Delivery** — Transport goods between cities
4. **Exploration** — Map unknown territories
5. **Labyrinth** — Explore and retrieve items/artifacts
6. **Information** — Gather intelligence
7. **Bodyguard** — Personal protection detail

## 4.4 Guild Politics
- The Guild is officially neutral — it operates across all kingdoms
- Guild secrets are protected — monster intelligence is valuable
- The Guild can blacklist adventurers — a death sentence for careers
- S-rank adventurers have significant political influence

# V. ECONOMIC SYSTEMS

## 5.1 Currency
- **Gold Coin (Large):** Highest standard, used for major transactions
- **Silver Coin:** Daily work, adventurer pay
- **Copper Coin:** Small purchases, commoner economy
- **Currency Exchange:** Each kingdom mints its own, but Asuran gold is the standard

## 5.2 Economic Sectors
1. **Agriculture:** Largest sector, Asura dominates
2. **Adventuring:** Monster parts, labyrinth treasures, quest rewards
3. **Magic Item Manufacturing:** Ranoa's specialty
4. **Crafting:** Dwarf-produced weapons and armor
5. **Trade:** Port cities, caravan routes
6. **Services:** Inns, blacksmiths, healers, teachers

## 5.3 Wealth Distribution
- **Top:** Asuran royalty, high nobles, S-rank adventurers
- **Upper:** Minor nobles, A-rank adventurers, master craftsmen, wealthy merchants
- **Middle:** B-C rank adventurers, skilled laborers, shopkeepers
- **Lower:** D-F rank adventurers, unskilled laborers, subsistence farmers
- **Bottom:** Slaves, refugees, disabled

# VI. SOCIAL HIERARCHY — DETAILED

## 6.1 The Three Estates
1. **Nobility (Blood)**
   - Royal family
   - High Nobility (Dukes, Marquises)
   - Middle Nobility (Counts, Viscounts)
   - Low Nobility (Barons, Knights)

2. **Commoner Meritocracy**
   - S-A rank Adventurers (rank supersedes birth)
   - Master Craftsmen
   - Guild Masters
   - Licensed Merchants

3. **Commoners**
   - Skilled Laborers
   - Soldiers
   - Farmers
   - Unskilled Laborers
   - Beggars

## 6.2 Social Mobility Paths
1. **Adventuring** — The most accessible path. Kill monsters, gain rank, earn respect.
2. **Magic Education** — Requires talent and money, but can elevate a commoner.
3. **Military Service** — Knight status can be earned through exceptional service.
4. **Marriage** — The classic noble path.
5. **Commerce** — Merchants can buy titles in some kingdoms.

# VII. MAGICAL TECHNOLOGY

## 7.1 Existing Magitech
- **Magic Circles:** Used for enchantment, barriers, teleportation
- **Magic Armor (Rudeus/Zanoba):** Golem-like exoskeleton powered by wearer's mana
- **Magic Eyes:** Implanted or inherited, grant special vision abilities
- **Magic Items:** Weapon enchantments, storage items, communication orbs
- **Magic Gems:** Store mana for later use

## 7.2 Technological Level (Non-Magical)
- **Transportation:** Horse-drawn carriages, sailing ships
- **Communication:** Letters, messenger birds, rare magical communication
- **Lighting:** Candles, oil lamps, magical light stones
- **Construction:** Stone and wood, magic-assisted for large structures
- **Medicine:** Healing magic > herbal medicine
- **Printing:** Limited, books are hand-copied

# VIII. NOTABLE WORLD LAWS AND CUSTOMS

## 8.1 The Milis Marriage Law
Monogamy enforced by the Milis Church. Polygamy is technically illegal in Milis-influenced regions but nobles often find loopholes.

## 8.2 The Adventurer's Right
Adventurers have legal authority to kill monsters in any jurisdiction. This is the foundation of their social utility.

## 8.3 Noble Duel Rights
Nobles can settle disputes through formal duels, though this is increasingly rare.

## 8.4 The Sea Race Treaty
Ships crossing the Ringus Sea must carry permits from the Sea Race or risk destruction. This is a hard geopolitical constraint.

# IX. GAME ADAPTATION NOTES

## 9.1 World as Progression System
The continents naturally form a difficulty curve:
1. Central Continent (Easy) — Safe zones, tutorial areas
2. Milis Continent (Medium) — Beast races, forest dungeons
3. Demon Continent (Hard) — Strong monsters, survival mechanics
4. Begaritt Continent (Very Hard) — Labyrinths, high mana chaos
5. Heaven Continent (Endgame) — Only accessible by the strongest

## 9.2 Zones as Biomes
Each continent has distinct biome identity that should visually and mechanically differentiate them in-game.

## 9.3 Faction System
- Asura Kingdom
- Milis Church
- Adventurer's Guild
- Ranoa Magic University
- Dragon King Kingdom
- Demon Kings
- Beast Tribes
- Sea Race

These provide alignment, quest sources, and reputation systems for gameplay.

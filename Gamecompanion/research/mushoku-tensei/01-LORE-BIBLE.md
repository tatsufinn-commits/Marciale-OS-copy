# I. COSMOLOGY — THE SIX-FACED WORLD

## 1.1 The Creator God and the Six Worlds
**Design Philosophy:** The cosmology is a closed, dying system. This creates inherent stakes — the world is already post-apocalyptic before the story begins.

**Verified Fact (LN Canon):**
- A primordial Creator God existed solely to create worlds
- Before dying, the Creator made one final, unbalanced world
- To stabilize it, He created five more and joined them like faces of a cube
- Each world had its own God to govern it:
  - Human World (Human God)
  - Dragon World (Dragon God)
  - Demon World (Demon God)
  - Beast World (Beast God)
  - Heaven World (Heaven God)
  - Sea World (Sea God)

- The six worlds are connected at the Void World — the empty space inside the cube where the original Dragon God Orsted and the Man-God (Hitogami) exert influence

## 1.2 The Dragon-God War — The First Cataclysm
**Why it matters:** This is the single most important historical event. Every geopolitical reality, racial tension, and power structure in the present day stems from this war.

- The First Dragon God waged war against the other worlds
- All the original Gods (except the Human God) died
- The Human God rallied survivors from each destroyed world and brought them through a portal to the Human World
- This is why multiple races co-exist on the Human World — they are refugees
- The original Dragon God's son, Orsted, is a central figure (demi-god)

## 1.3 The Six-Faced World Today
The story primarily takes place on the **Human World** face, now a single cube-face continent with five major landmasses plus surrounding oceans ruled by the Sea Race.

# II. MAGIC SYSTEM — THE CORE MECHANIC

## 2.1 Magic Fundamentals
**Design Philosophy:** Magic is a skill, not a birthright. It follows learnable rules. This makes it game-adaptable as a progression system.

**Verified Fact (LN Canon):**
- Magic is cast through **incantations** — spoken words that shape mana
- **Voiceless Incantation** is an extremely rare ability (only Rudeus and Orsted possess it naturally in the present era) — it allows casting without chanting through deep understanding of magic's principles
- All humans have mana, but quantity varies by race
- Magic requires: Mana + Understanding + Incantation (or equivalent mental shaping)
- Chanting = safety, precision, standardized power output
- Voiceless = speed, flexibility, requires deep conceptual mastery

## 2.2 Magic Disciplines (Attack Magic)
1. **Water Magic** — Ice, water pressure, healing derivatives
2. **Fire Magic** — Heat, explosions, combustion
3. **Earth Magic** — Stone, metal manipulation, terrain shaping
4. **Wind Magic** — Air pressure, cutting winds, flight assistance
5. **Healing Magic** — Wound closure, detoxification, disease curing
6. **Detoxification Magic** — Poison and ailment removal
7. **Divine Magic** — Higher-order purification, barrier magic
8. **Barrier Magic** — Protective wards, sealing
9. **Summoning Magic** — Rare, largely lost to history
10. **Unique Magic** — Personal variants (Disturb Magic, Howling, Gravity, Demon Eye magic)

## 2.3 Magic Ranking System
**Design Philosophy:** Ranks are *descriptive*, not *prescriptive*. They measure demonstrated capability, not potential. This creates natural progression goals.

| Rank | Offensive Magic Scale | Support/Healing Scale |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Elementary** | Fairly harmless | Minor wound healing |
| **Intermediate** | Good damage output | Poison cure, serious wounds |
| **Advanced** | Considerable damage, environmental destruction | Broken bones, wide poison cure |
| **Saint** | Small-town destruction | Life-threatening wounds, most poisons |
| **King** | Army-level devastation | Limb regeneration, advanced disease cure |
| **Imperial** | Kingdom/region nuke | Near-death revival, lost limb regrowth |
| **God** | Continental scale, physics defiance | Incurable diseases, conceptual healing |

**Critical Nuance:** Mages are titled by their *highest demonstrated spell*, not overall ability. Rudeus is officially a "Water Saint-tier Magician" (knows one Saint-tier Water spell: Aqua Heal/Healing) but can amplify Elementary-Advanced spells to Emperor-level power through his massive mana pool and voiceless incantation technique.

**Naming Convention:** "[Element] [Rank]-tier Magician" — e.g., Roxy is a "Water Saint-tier Magician"

## 2.4 The Mana Factor
- Races born in high-mana environments (Demon Continent) have naturally higher mana pools
- Rudeus's mana pool is monstrous — 2-4x a normal mage — because he trained it from infancy
- Chantless magic uses more mana per spell but allows rapid-fire casting
- Mana can be visualized, sensed, and measured by experienced mages

## 2.5 Magic Items and Enchantment
- Magic circles are used for complex enchantments
- Magic items require mana to activate
- "Magic Armor" is a unique creation by Rudeus and Zanoba — a golem-like exoskeleton powered by the wearer's mana
- Magic eyes (Demon Eyes) grant special abilities (clairvoyance, foresight, x-ray)
- Magic gems / jewels can store mana

# III. SWORDSMANSHIP — THE THREE STYLES

## 3.1 Design Philosophy
Each sword style embodies a distinct **combat philosophy**. They are not just fighting techniques — they are worldviews. This is critical for character design: a character's chosen style reflects their personality.

## 3.2 The Three Great Sword Styles

### Sword God Style
- **Founder:** Sword God (unknown ancient)
- **Current God:** Gal Farion → Gino Britts
- **Philosophy:** Overwhelming speed and offense. End the fight in one blow.
- **Signature Technique:** Longsword of Light — a strike so fast it appears as a flash
- **Progression:** Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced → Saint → King → Emperor → God
- **Key Ability:** Battle Aura (Touki) — first achieved at Advanced rank, enhances physical capabilities
- **Notable Practitioners:** Ghislaine (Sword King), Eris (Sword King/Emperor potential), Gal Farion

### Water God Style
- **Founder:** Water God (also mastered Divine-tier water magic)
- **Current God:** Reida Reia
- **Philosophy:** Ultimate defense. Parry, deflect, redirect. Flow like water.
- **Signature Technique:** Flow (deflection art) + Sword of Deprivation Kingdom (6th Secret Art)
- **Nature:** Reactive, counter-based. Patient. Reads the opponent.
- **Notable Practitioners:** Reida Reia, Isolte Cruel

### North God Style
- **Founder:** North God Kalman I
- **Current God:** Aleksander Rybak (Kalman III)
- **Philosophy:** Pragmatic, adaptive, without fixed rules. Use environment, trickery, unconventional weapons, anything.
- **Signature Aspect:** No fixed techniques — subjective mastery across all methods
- **Nature:** The "joker" style. Highly individual. Adapts to the practitioner.
- **Notable Practitioners:** Kalman I, Kalman II (Sandor), Kalman III, Auber, Ruijerd (adapted), Doga

## 3.3 Sword Ranking System

| Rank | Sword God Style Marker | General Significance |
|------|----------------------|--------------------|
| **Basic** | Learning stance/grips | Can hold a sword |
| **Intermediate** | Can fight | Average soldier level |
| **Advanced** | Battle Aura unlocked | S-Rank adventurer, top 5% of warriors |
| **Saint** | Can use Longsword of Light (SG) | Regional hero, Paul's effective level |
| **King** | Can face two Saints equally (SG) | National asset, ~50 people globally |
| **Emperor** | Defeat a King with witnesses | World power, extremely rare |
| **God** | Defeat the current God | One per style, pinnacle of humanity |

**Note:** "Sword God" can refer to both the rank and the specific style's leader — a deliberate source of in-world confusion.

**Community Consensus:** There are approximately 50 King-rank or higher individuals in the entire world at any given time.

# V. WORLD GEOGRAPHY

## 5.1 The Five Continents

### Central Continent
- **Size:** Largest continent
- **Climate:** Temperate, cold north, moderate south
- **Dominant Race:** Humans
- **Key Feature:** Red Dragon Mountain Range — splits the continent into three zones
- **Three Zones:**
  1. **North** — Cold, poor, war-torn. Contains the Magic Triumvirate (Ranoa, Neris, Basherant)
  2. **West** — The Asura Kingdom — world's richest, most fertile land
  3. **South** — Conflict Zone — constant warfare, short-lived kingdoms

### Demon Continent
- **Size:** Second largest
- **Climate:** Harsh, high mana concentration, strong monsters
- **Dominant Race:** Demon Race (魔族 — Ma-zoku)
- **Key Feature:** Extremely intelligent monsters that learn from attacks
- **Government:** Demon Kings rule various territories
- **Culture:** Lawless, survivalist, yet has trading ports
- **Economic Reality:** Poor, but strategically vital for trade routes

### Milis Continent
- **Size:** Third largest
- **Climate:** Northern jungle (Great Forest), Southern temperate
- **Dominant Race:** Human/Beast Race (~50/50)
- **Key Feature:** Blue Dragon Mountain Range with the Holy Sword Highway
- **Holy Milis Kingdom:** World's second most powerful nation
- **Note:** Only continent with a monster-free highway

### Begaritt Continent
- **Size:** Fourth largest
- **Climate:** Arid, mana-dense, labyrinth-riddled
- **Dominant Race:** Mixed — mostly adventurers and ex-adventurers
- **Key Feature:** Highest concentration of labyrinths in the world
- **Monster Strength:** Equal to Demon Continent

### Heaven Continent
- **Size:** Smallest
- **Elevation:** ~3000 meters above sea level (3.6x Burj Khalifa)
- **Dominant Race:** Heaven Race (winged humanoids)
- **Accessibility:** Nearly impossible to reach from ground
- **Key Feature:** The labyrinth called "Hell"

## 5.2 Oceans and Seas
- **Ringus Sea** — Dominated by the Sea Race, extremely dangerous without permits
- The Sea Race sinks unauthorized vessels — makes intercontinental travel politically complex

# VI. KINGDOMS & POLITICS

## 6.1 Asura Kingdom (World's Strongest)
- **Government:** Feudal monarchy with concubine palace politics
- **Capital:** Arusu/Als
- **Economy:** Extreme agricultural wealth — land is fertile, population is fed
- **Military:** Weak monsters = weak military necessity. Relies on adventurers.
- **Key Region:** Fittoa Territory (Fedoa) — border region, ruled by the Boreas Greyrat family
- **Power Structure:** King → Nobility (Dukes, Marquises, Counts) → Knights → Adventurers → Peasants
- **Currency:** Asuran gold coins (standardized)

## 6.2 Holy Milis Kingdom (World's Second)
- **Government:** Theocracy — Milis Church is the state religion
- **Capital:** Milishion
- **Economy:** Religious tithes, agriculture, trade
- **Key Feature:** Adventurer's Guild headquarters located here
- **Social Control:** Milis Church enforces strict monogamy, suppresses demon races

## 6.3 Dragon King Kingdom (World's Third)
- **Government:** Military monarchy
- **Location:** Southern Central Continent
- **Key Feature:** Strong military, vassal states (Shiron, Sanakia, Kikka)

## 6.4 Ranoa Kingdom (Magic Nation)
- **Government:** Magocracy — ruled by mages
- **Economy:** Magic item manufacturing, education
- **Key Feature:** Ranoa Magic University — premier magical institution
- **Part of:** Magic Triumvirate (Ranoa, Neris, Basherant)

- The Milis Church preaches human supremacy
- Rudeus unintentionally challenges these prejudices through his actions

# VIII. GUILDS & ORGANIZATIONS

## 8.1 Adventurer's Guild
- **Headquarters:** Milishion, Milis Continent
- **Purpose:** Monster subjugation, exploration, escort, delivery
- **Ranking:** S → A → B → C → D → E → F (S is highest)
- **Function:** Quests posted, completed, rank increases with achievements
- **Economic Role:** Primary employment for combat-capable individuals
- **Social Role:** Equalizer — rank matters more than birth

## 8.2 Milis Church
- **Headquarters:** Milishion
- **Beliefs:** Worship of "God" (Milis) — one god monotheism
- **Social Control:** Enforces monogamy, anti-demon rhetoric
- **Political Power:** Second most powerful nation
- **Contradiction:** Preaches peace, practices discrimination

## 8.3 Magic University of Ranoa
- **Location:** Ranoa Kingdom, Central Continent
- **Purpose:** Magical education and research
- **Structure:** Regular students + Special Students (combat-capable)
- **Social Dynamic:** Mix of nobles and commoners, but class divides persist
- **Curriculum:** Magic theory, practical casting, enchantment, languages

## 8.4 The Seven Knights of Asura
- Elite knights serving the Asura royal family
- Includes: Sword Emperor, Water King Isolte, North Emperor Doga
- Political tool of the king

# IX. ECONOMY & TECHNOLOGY

## 9.1 Economic Layers
- **Subsistence Agriculture** — Most of the world
- **Feudal Extraction** — Asura Kingdom's model
- **Adventurer Economy** — Quest-based, risk-driven
- **Mage Economy** — Magic items, enchantment, research grants

## 9.2 Technology Level
- **Pre-Industrial:** No electricity, steam power, or combustion engines
- **Medieval Fantasy baseline:** Candles, lamps, horse carriages, swords
- **Advanced Magic Items:** Exist but rare and expensive
- **Communication:** Letter-based, magical long-distance communication is rare
- **Printing:** Limited — books are valuable

## 9.3 Currency
- Gold, Silver, and Copper coins
- Asuran currency is the most trusted standard
- Adventurer Guild handles financial transactions between parties

# XIV. SOCIAL HIERARCHY

```
King / Emperor
  ↓
Nobility (Duke → Marquis → Count → Viscount → Baron)
  ↓
Knights (Status Nobility, not Blood Nobility)
  ↓
Adventurers (Ranked S→F)
  ↓
Merchants & Artisans
  ↓
Commoners (Farmers, Laborers)
  ↓
Demon Race / Beast Race (Socially Lower)
  ↓
Slaves (Legal in some regions)
```

**Key Insight:** Adventurer rank can supersede birth status. An S-rank adventurer of common birth commands more respect than a minor noble. This is Rudeus's primary social mobility tool.

# XV. MONSTERS

## 15.1 Monster Ecology
**Design Philosophy:** Monsters are not random — they are environmental. A region's monsters reflect its mana density and history.

- Monsters are stronger in high-mana environments (Demon Continent > Begaritt > Milis > Central)
- The Red Dragon Mountain Range has the strongest monsters on Central Continent
- Asura Kingdom has the weakest monsters (C-rank at most)
- Monsters on the Demon Continent learn from attacks — repeat a tactic and they counter it

## 15.2 Monster Ranking
Monsters are ranked F through S, matching Adventurer Guild difficulty:
| Rank | Threat Level | Adventurer Required |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| F | Harmless | Any |
| E | Minor | Any |
| D | Novice | E-rank+ |
| C | Intermediate | D-rank+ |
| B | Advanced | C-rank+ |
| A | Dangerous | B-rank+ |
| S | Catastrophic | A-rank+ |

## 15.3 Notable Monster Types
- Red Dragons — Dragon King's Mountain Range, very dangerous
- Hydra — King-level threat, appears in Begaritt
- Undead — Labyrinth common enemies
- Golems — Magical constructs, labyrinth guardians
- Chimera — Rare, dangerous hybrids

# XVIII. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY SUMMARY

**What makes the Mushoku Tensei world work as a setting for adaptation:**

1. **Closed, Dying System** — The world is post-apocalyptic from the start. Gods are dead. Refugees live in diaspora. This creates immediate stakes.

2. **Meritocratic Undertow** — Adventurer ranking, sword ranks, and magic tiers all provide measurable progression that a player can understand and pursue.

3. **Racism is Real** — Discrimination isn't flavor text. It affects character motivations (Ruijerd's curse, Migurd isolation) and creates conflict.

4. **Power has Names, not Numbers** — "Sword King" means more than "Level 50." The Seven Great Powers are named individuals, not abstract tiers.

5. **Ranks are Descriptive, Not Prescriptive** — A "Water Saint-tier" mage can be weaker or stronger than the title suggests. This allows flexibility.

6. **Consequences are Permanent** — Paul dies. Eris leaves. Zenith is catatonic. The world doesn't bend to protect characters.

7. **Knowledge is Power** — Rudeus's Earth knowledge (physics, chemistry, tactics) is as valuable as his mana pool.

# I. KEY SCENES

## Scene 1: The Prologue (Vol 1)

*Rudeus's past life. A 34-year-old NEET, disowned, homeless. He dies saving three teenagers from a truck.*

> **RUDEUS (internal):** If I could start over from the beginning...
>
> *The truck hits. Light. Then nothing.*
>
> *Then... light again. A woman's face. Warm. Concerned.*
>
> **ZENITH:** Oh, thank goodness. He's awake!
>
> *Rudeus tries to speak. Only a baby's cry comes out.*
>
> *He has been given a second chance.*

**Narrative purpose:** Establishes Rudeus's past life trauma. Every achievement in the game is set against this failure.

---

## Scene 2: The First Magic Lesson (Vol 1, Chapter 3)

*Roxy teaches 3-year-old Rudeus magic.*

> **ROXY:** Now, concentrate. Imagine water flowing from your palm. Don't force it — guide it.
>
> *Rudeus concentrates. A small ball of water forms above his hand.*
>
> **ROXY:** ...Already?
>
> **RUDEUS:** Is that bad?
>
> **ROXY:** No. No, that's not bad at all. *smiles* You're a natural.
>
> *She doesn't know he's been practicing the chant in his head for a week.*
>
> **RUDEUS:** Teach me more.
>
> **ROXY:** *laughs* Eager, aren't you? Alright. Let's see how far you can go.

**Narrative purpose:** Establishes Rudeus's talent and Roxy's teaching philosophy — patient, encouraging, structured.

---

## Scene 3: Meeting Eris (Vol 2)

*Rudeus arrives at the Boreas Greyrat mansion to tutor Eris. She attacks him immediately.*

> **ERIS:** What the heck? Aren't you smaller than me!? Are you joking to let someone like this teach me!?
>
> **RUDEUS:** I think that doesn't have anything to do with age.
>
> **ERIS:** What did you say!? You dare to argue with me!?
>
> *She slaps him.*
>
> **RUDEUS:** ...Then, I'm hitting back.
>
> *He slaps her. She explodes.*
>
> *The chase begins.*

**Narrative purpose:** Establishes Eris's violent nature and Rudeus's refusal to be a doormat. Their relationship starts in conflict.

---

## Scene 4: Turning Point 2 — Orsted (Vol 4)

*At the Red Dragon's Upper Jaw. Orsted appears.*

> **ORSTED:** You are Rudeus Greyrat. You have been manipulated by Hitogami.
>
> **RUDEUS:** What? Who are you?
>
> *Orsted doesn't answer. He attacks.*
>
> *Rudeus casts everything he has. All of it. Every spell. Every trick.*
>
> *Orsted absorbs it all with Dragon Gate.*
>
> **ORSTED:** You rely too much on magic. And your magic is too predictable.
>
> *A single strike. Rudeus falls.*
>
> **ORSTED:** You are weak. But that can change. If you survive.
>
> *He leaves. Rudeus lies in the dirt, broken, humiliated, alive.*

**Narrative purpose:** First real defeat. Rudeus discovers there are powers far beyond him, and that someone is manipulating events from the shadows.

---

## Scene 5: Paul's Death (Vol 6)

*The Teleport Labyrinth. Paul pushes Rudeus out of the way of the Guardian Beast's attack.*

> **PAUL:** RUDEUS! *pushes him*
>
> *The blow lands on Paul. He falls.*
>
> **RUDEUS:** Dad! No, no, no —
>
> **PAUL:** *coughs blood* ...I was... a bad father. I know that.
>
> **RUDEUS:** Stop talking! I'll heal you!
>
> **PAUL:** Listen to me. *grabs Rudeus's arm* You... take care of them. Your mother. Your sisters. The family.
>
> *His grip weakens.*
>
> **PAUL:** I'm proud of you, Rudeus. You became... a better man than I ever was.
>
> *He goes still.*
>
> *Rudeus screams.*

**Narrative purpose:** The end of childhood. Paul's death is the first permanent loss. Rudeus must now be the man of the family.

---

## Scene 6: The Reunion — Sylphy (Vol 9)

*At the Ranoa Magic University. Rudeus realizes "Fitz" is Sylphy.*

> **RUDEUS:** Sylphy?
>
> *Fitz freezes. The hood falls back. Green hair. Pointed ears. Tears.*
>
> **SYLPHY:** ...You recognized me.
>
> **RUDEUS:** Of course I did. I'd recognize you anywhere.
>
> *She throws herself at him. Crying.*
>
> **SYLPHY:** I thought I'd never see you again. When the Calamity happened, I thought —
>
> **RUDEUS:** I know. I know. I'm here. I'm not going anywhere.
>
> *They hold each other. Years of separation, healed in a moment.*

**Narrative purpose:** Emotional payoff. The first happy reunion in a story full of loss. Sylphy's faith in Rudeus is rewarded.

---

## Scene 7: Eris's Return (Vol 15)

*After years of training, Eris returns to Rudeus.*

> *Eris stands at the door of the Greyrat household. She looks different — taller, scarred, a sword at her hip. A Sword King.*
>
> **ERIS:** I'm back.
>
> *Long pause. She struggles.*
>
> **ERIS:** I... I was stupid. Leaving like that. The letter. I couldn't... I didn't know how to say it.
>
> **RUDEUS:** Say what?
>
> **ERIS:** *grits teeth, forces the words out* That I love you. That I wanted to be worthy of you. That I was scared you'd leave me first.
>
> *Rudeus steps forward.*
>
> **RUDEUS:** You've always been worthy, Eris. You just had to see it yourself.
>
> *She breaks down. He holds her.*

**Narrative purpose:** Eris's arc complete — from violent child to disciplined warrior to vulnerable woman. Her love is no longer expressed through fighting.

# I. PROTAGONIST

## Name: TBD by Player (Default: "Cael")
**Role:** The Weaver — player character
**Past Life:** An ordinary person who failed at their ambitions, died saving strangers, and was reincarnated

**Core Wound (MT-Inspired):** *"I never finished anything."* In their past life, the protagonist had talent but quit everything the moment they weren't the best. Their second chance is defined by their determination to *follow through*.

**Character Arc:**
- Early: Compulsive overachiever (trying to "win" at life through skill accumulation)
- Mid: Confronts limits — can't save everyone, can't be perfect
- Late: Learns that success is measured by the people who stand with you, not personal achievement

**Player Expression:** The protagonist has a defined personality (earnest, analytical, prone to self-doubt) but the player makes key dialogue choices that shape their moral alignment (Pragmatic / Compassionate / Reckless).

# II. COMPANIONS (Party Members)

## Vaela — The Echo (Half-Elf)

**Class:** Channeler (Ranged Aether Magic DPS)
**Age:** Appears 12-14 (half-elf, ages slower)
**Role:** Childhood friend, emotional anchor

**Psychology (MT-Inspired — Sylphiette's template):**
- **Core Wound:** Bullied for her half-elven heritage. Village children called her "point-ear" and "half-blood." She internalized that she is unlovable.
- **Core Drive:** Belonging. She will do anything to stay with people who accept her.
- **Flaw:** Apologetic. She takes blame that isn't hers. She defers to others even when she's right.
- **Growth:** Learning to stand up for herself, to have opinions, to be worth loving *without* earning it.

**Relationship Preferences:**
- Bonded with: Cael (first friend), Sera (intellectual kinship)
- Clashes with: Kaelen (too aggressive), Mira (too smothering)

**Personal Quest: "The Roots of Belonging"**
1. Why I Don't Fit — Flashback to her childhood bullying
2. The Telepathic Truth — Discover her mother left a message (Echo)
3. The Half-Blood Village — Visit a community of half-elves
4. Accepting the Difference — Choose: embrace her heritage or conceal it
5. The Family She Chose — She formally declares the party her family

**Why She Works:** She is soft, but not weak. When her friends are threatened, she acts with terrifying precision. Her kindness is earned — she could have become bitter. She chose not to.

## Kaelen — The Oathbound (Human)

**Class:** Striker (Melee Physical DPS)
**Age:** Late 20s
**Role:** The veteran, the big brother of the party

**Psychology (MT-Inspired — Eris's combat drive + Paul's responsibility arc):**
- **Core Wound:** He failed. As a royal guard, he was responsible for a prince who was assassinated. He was exiled. He carries the guilt of a life he couldn't save.
- **Core Drive:** Redemption through protection. He will never fail again.
- **Flaw:** Self-destructive. He takes risks that endanger himself because he subconsciously believes he deserves punishment.
- **Growth:** Learning that protecting others means protecting himself too.

**Relationship Preferences:**
- Bonded with: Mira (she heals him literally and figuratively)
- Clashes with: Vaela (reminds him of the prince he failed — makes him overprotective)

**Personal Quest: "The Knight's Redemption"**
1. The Name I Left Behind — Flashback to the prince's death
2. The Traitor's Trail — Investigate who really ordered the assassination
3. Atonement or Punishment — Confront the real traitor
4. The Oath Renewed — Choose: forgive himself or continue atonement
5. A New Kingdom to Serve — Swear loyalty to the party, not a crown

**Why He Works:** He is the most physically capable party member, but his greatest enemy is his own guilt. His strength is also his vulnerability.

## Sera — The Echo Walker (Human)

**Class:** Shaper (Utility/Control Mage)
**Age:** Mid 20s
**Role:** The scholar, the lore expert, the one who asks "why?"

**Psychology (MT-Inspired — Roxy's intellectual curiosity + loneliness):**
- **Core Wound:** Her mentor disappeared. She spent years searching, only to find he had been consumed by an Echo — lost in the past. She fears the same fate.
- **Core Drive:** Understanding. She believes if she learns enough, she can prevent tragedy.
- **Flaw:** Detached. She intellectualizes emotional situations, keeping people at arm's length.
- **Growth:** Learning that some things cannot be understood — only felt.

**Relationship Preferences:**
- Bonded with: Vaela (genuine curiosity about half-elf culture)
- Clashes with: Rynn (too chaotic, too emotional)

**Personal Quest: "The Echo Seeker's Truth"**
1. The Disappeared — Search for her mentor
2. The Forbidden Echo — Decipher the Echo that consumed him
3. The Warning Within — Learn the truth: he chose to stay in the Echo
4. Letting Go — Visit his Echo one last time to say goodbye
5. The New Archive — Build a library of Echoes as his memorial

**Why She Works:** She is the smartest person in the room, and the loneliest. Her knowledge is her armor, but it also isolates her.

## Mira — The Mender (Human)

**Class:** Mender (Healer/Support)
**Age:** Early 20s
**Role:** The medic, the heart, the one who holds the party together

**Psychology (MT-Inspired — Elinalise's maternal care + survivor's guilt):**
- **Core Wound:** She was a battlefield medic. Her entire squad was wiped out. She survived because she was behind cover. She believes she should have died with them.
- **Core Drive:** Never lose anyone again. She heals obsessively.
- **Flaw:** Codependent. She neglects her own health to care for others.
- **Growth:** Learning that she deserves to be healed too.

**Relationship Preferences:**
- Bonded with: Kaelen (they share survivor's guilt)
- Clashes with: Kaelen (their shared guilt can be toxic if unchecked)

**Personal Quest: "The Healer's Reckoning"**
1. The Faces I See — Flashback to her squad's last stand
2. The Survivor's Trail — Find the family of a fallen squadmate
3. The Unhealed Wound — Confront her guilt in an Echo vision
4. The Permission to Live — A squadmate's Echo tells her: "Live for us"
5. The New Vow — Heal not from guilt, but from hope

**Why She Works:** She is the kindest person in the party, but her kindness is built on trauma. Her healing is a form of atonement until she learns to heal for its own sake.

## Rynn — The Last Summoner (Wisp-Touched Human)

**Class:** Wisp (Summoner/Debuffer)
**Age:** Appears 16 (actually unknown — time distortion in Maelstrom)
**Role:** The wildcard, the mysterious one, the outsider

**Psychology (MT-Inspired — Ruijerd's curse + Zanoba's obsession):**
- **Core Wound:** She was trapped alone in the Maelstrom's edge for years. Time moved differently. She aged mentally but not physically. She survived by binding aether constructs as companions.
- **Core Drive:** Connection. She has been alone so long she craves contact but doesn't know how to handle it.
- **Flaw:** Socially maladjusted. She says inappropriate things, invades personal space, and doesn't understand boundaries.
- **Growth:** Learning that she can be loved as herself, not just tolerated.

**Relationship Preferences:**
- Bonded with: Nobody initially (too strange). Gradually bonds with Sera (mutual curiosity).
- Clashes with: Everyone initially. Her arc is about earning their trust.

**Personal Quest: "The Last Summoner"**
1. The Girl in the Maelstrom — Learn her backstory
2. The Construct's Heart — Her oldest aether construct is failing
3. The Binding Ritual — She must recharge it, costing her memories
4. The Choice — Sacrifice the construct or let it die
5. The First Friend — She chooses connection over isolation

**Why She Works:** She is uncomfortable to be around. That's the point. Her growth is visible through the party's changing reactions to her.

## Elathor, The Eternal Weaver (Final Boss — MT's Orsted)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Role | Primary antagonist → reluctant ally → sacrifice/final boss |
| Age | Timeless (created at the dawn of the Loom) |
| Personality | Exhausted, calculating, tired of cycles, desperate |
| Fighting Style | Counters everything the player has learned — forces mastery |
| Purpose | The ultimate test: both mechanical and philosophical |

**Design Philosophy (MT's Orsted template):**
Elathor is not evil. He is *tired*. He has lived through tens of thousands of cycles, each ending in failure. He has used Weavers as tools. He has made allies into sacrifices. He has done terrible things for a cause he believes is just.

The player's journey is to find a *third path* — one Elathor never considered because he stopped believing it was possible.

**Boss Fight Design (see Monster Bible for mechanics):**
- Phase 1: Tests raw combat skill (can you survive?)
- Phase 2: Tests system mastery (can you use everything you've learned?)
- Phase 3: Tests narrative understanding (do you understand what he's been through?)

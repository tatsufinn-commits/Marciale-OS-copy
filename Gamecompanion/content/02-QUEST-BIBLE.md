# I. QUEST DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

1. **Every quest serves the narrative, a system unlock, or a character moment.**
2. **No fetch quests without context.** If you're collecting 10 wolf pelts, there's a reason that matters.
3. **Quests are optional but rewarding.** Missing a quest means missing lore or gear, not content gating.
4. **Personal quests are the heart of the relationship system.** They drive Affinity.

# I. PARTY MEMBERS (Player-Controlled)

## Rudeus Greyrat — The Quagmire

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Class (Gameplay)** | Versatile Mage / Magic Armor User |
| **LN Role** | Protagonist, former NEET reincarnated |
| **Age Range** | 0 → 15 (Vol 1-15) |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- **Core Wound:** "I never finished anything." A gifted child who became a NEET after trauma. His second chance is defined by his terror of repeating past failures.
- **Motivation:** Live a life without regrets. Protect his found family.
- **Flaw:** Overthinks, prone to depression when he fails someone.
- **Growth:** From compensatory overachiever → genuine adult who values connection over accomplishment.

**Gameplay Role:**
- **Primary:** Ranged Magic DPS (Water, Earth, Fire, Wind)
- **Secondary:** Magic Armor user (tank mode in Mk I/0)
- **Special:** Voiceless Incantation — faster cast speed than other mages
- **Signature Skill:** Quagmire (Water + Earth combo — AoE slow + damage)

**Personal Quest:** "The Oath" — 5 chapters covering his journey from NEET to family man.

## Sylphiette — The Silent Fitts

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Class (Gameplay)** | Stealth Mage / Support |
| **LN Role** | Rudeus's childhood friend, first wife |
| **Age Range** | 5 → 16 (Vol 1-15) |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- **Core Wound:** Abandonment anxiety. Bullied for half-elf heritage, internalized that she is unlovable. Rudeus proved otherwise.
- **Motivation:** Stay with Rudeus forever. Protect him from the shadows.
- **Flaw:** Apologetic, defers to others, hides her own needs.
- **Growth:** From bullied child → confident protector → nurturing matriarch.

**Gameplay Role:**
- **Primary:** Multi-element mage (all elements Advanced-tier)
- **Specialty:** Voiceless Incantation + stealth (Fitz persona)
- **Support:** Barrier magic, healing, buffs
- **Signature Skill:** Wind barrier + silent casting

**Personal Quest:** "The Silent Promise" — 5 chapters: childhood, the calamity, survival, Fitz, reunion.

## Roxy Migurdia — The Water Saint

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Class (Gameplay)** | Pure Mage / Water Specialist |
| **LN Role** | Rudeus's first teacher, second wife |
| **Age** | ~40+ (Migurd physiology: appears young) |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- **Core Wound:** "I succeeded, and was punished for it." A prodigy in her village, she was exiled for being too talented. She has been wandering ever since.
- **Motivation:** Help others grow. Teaching is her identity.
- **Flaw:** Avoidant of confrontation, lonely but afraid to connect.
- **Growth:** From rootless wanderer → anchored wife and mother.

**Gameplay Role:**
- **Primary:** Water Magic DPS — long-range artillery
- **Specialty:** Aqua Heartia staff (×5 water magic multiplier)
- **Support:** Healing water magic
- **Signature Skill:** Water Storm Saint-tier spell

**Personal Quest:** "The Wanderer's Home" — 5 chapters: leaving Migurd village, teaching, loneliness, rescue, belonging.

## Eris Boreas Greyrat — The Mad Sword King

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Class (Gameplay)** | Melee DPS / Sword God Style |
| **LN Role** | Rudeus's first student, first love, third wife |
| **Age Range** | 8 → 20 (Vol 1-15) |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- **Core Wound:** "I am not worthy of love unless I am strong." Raised to believe her value is in her Boreas blood — fierce, proud, powerful.
- **Motivation:** Become strong enough to protect Rudeus. Her way of loving.
- **Flaw:** Self-destructive. Takes risks because she subconsciously believes she deserves punishment for her past violence.
- **Growth:** From violent brute → disciplined sword king → woman who can love without fighting.

**Gameplay Role:**
- **Primary:** Melee DPS — single-target execution
- **Specialty:** Sword God Style — highest crit damage in the game
- **Signature:** Longsword of Light — instant, unavoidable damage
- **Unique mechanic:** Rage — builds through combat, unleashes devastating finishers

**Personal Quest:** "The Sword and the Heart" — 5 chapters: first meeting, Demon Continent, departure, training, return.

## Zanoba Shirone — The Doll Prince

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Class (Gameplay)** | Engineer / Crafter / Support |
| **LN Role** | Rudeus's best friend, business partner |
| **Age** | ~16-20 |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- **Core Wound:** "Nothing in the real world is as perfect as what I can create." Disengaged from politics and normal life because human affairs are messy.
- **Motivation:** Create the perfect figure/automaton.
- **Flaw:** Obsessive. His devotion to craftsmanship sometimes blinds him to human needs.
- **Growth:** From isolated genius → loyal friend who uses his talents for others.

**Gameplay Role:**
- **Primary:** Backline support — summons doll constructs
- **Specialty:** Crafting bonuses (faster forge leveling, better fuse odds)
- **Unique:** Co-creator of Magic Armor — enables special equipment crafting
- **Signature:** Doll King's Command — summon multiple dolls to tank/distract

**Personal Quest:** "The Perfect Form" — 5 chapters: exile from Shirone, friendship, Magic Armor, purpose, legacy.

## Orsted — The Dragon God (Final Boss)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Role** | Antagonist → Ally, Final Boss (training battle) |
| **First Appears** | Chapter 10 (Turning Point 2) |
| **Final Battle** | Chapter 40 |

**Psychology (from Character Bible):**
- Orsted has been fighting Hitogami across timelines for thousands of years
- He is exhausted, calculating, ruthless — but not evil
- He has killed Rudeus in previous timelines
- His arc in Vol 1-15 is from enemy → reluctant teacher → ally

**Boss Fight Structure (Chapter 40):**
- **Phase 1:** Tests raw combat — Orsted uses Dragon God Touki, absorbs magic
- **Phase 2:** Tests mastery — Orsted adapts to player strategies mid-fight
- **Phase 3:** Tests resolve — narrative choice: does Rudeus prove he is not a tool?

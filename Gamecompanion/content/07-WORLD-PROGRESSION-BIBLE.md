# IV. BALANCING ROADMAP

## 4.1 Launch Balance Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Time to complete main story | ~60 hours (active) |
| Time to max level (100) | ~300 hours (active + idle) |
| Time to full endgame gear | ~150 hours of optimized farming |
| Progression walls (require grinding) | 3-4 points in main story |
| Offline reward as % of active | 60% (capped 12h) |
| Party completion (all companions at Bonded) | ~80 hours |
| All achievements | ~400 hours |

## 4.2 Identified Balance Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Gold inflation** — Late-game gold earn rates trivialize sink costs | Dynamic sink scaling: costs scale with player gold/hour |
| **Crafting dominates drops** — Players only craft, never equip drops | Attunement node: "Loot Sense" — +drop rate for unequipped slots |
| **One build is mathematically best** — All players converge on same party | Resistance types, affinity interactions, and encounter-specific mechanics |
| **Offline feels mandatory** — Players feel punished for not checking every 12h | 60% efficiency cap means active play is always better |
| **Relationship system ignored** — Players min-max without engaging personal quests | Affinity bonuses are substantial enough to matter; departure mechanic creates cost for neglect |

## 4.3 Post-Launch Balance Philosophy

| Priority | Category | Approach |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Bugs | Fix within 24-48 hours |
| 2 | Exploits | Patch immediately, communicate transparently |
| 3 | Class balance | Review monthly, adjust based on pick rate + performance data |
| 4 | Economy drift | Adjust earn rates quarterly |
| 5 | New content | Major content drops every 3-4 months |
| 6 | Nerfs | Nerf only when a strategy invalidates others; buff alternatives first |

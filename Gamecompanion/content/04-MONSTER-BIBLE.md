# I. MONSTER DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

1. **Every monster tells a story.** Riftspawn are not random — they are aether-corrupted versions of real creatures.
2. **Difficulty is in patterns, not stats.** A well-designed monster can be threatening without inflated numbers.
3. **Bosses teach lessons.** Each boss tests a specific skill the player should have learned in that zone.

# III. FINAL BOSS: ELATHOR, THE ETERNAL WEAVER

**Zone:** The Heart of the Maelstrom
**Level:** 100
**Dependencies:** All 6 personal quests completed (recommended), 280 stages cleared, full attunement

## Phase 1 — The Eternal's Judgment
*"Show me what you have learned."*

**Mechanics:**
- Elathor cycles through 6 attack patterns based on the 6 Weaver classes
- Each pattern counters a specific strategy:
  - Warden pattern: Punishes clustering (AoE shield-breaker)
  - Striker pattern: Punishes standing still (rapid strikes)
  - Channeler pattern: Punishes kiting (homing bolts)
  - Mender pattern: Punishes ignoring debuffs (heal reversal)
  - Shaper pattern: Punishes predictable positioning (arena reshaping)
  - Wisp pattern: Punishes focusing boss (summoned copies)
- **Pass:** Player must adapt their formation and skills on the fly

## Phase 2 — The Cycle Unbroken
*"I have done this ten thousand times. You are the first to reach me."*

**Mechanics:**
- Elathor creates 4 Echo copies of the party
- Player fights their own team's stats
- Debuff: "Cycle Fatigue" — damage reduction decreases over time
- Adds: Previous party compositions (ghosts of past Weavers)
- **Pass:** Player must defeat their own Echoes while Elathor regenerates

## Phase 3 — The Final Thread
*"Break the cycle. Or join it."*

**Mechanics:**
- Elathor becomes a multi-target boss (3 nodes: Memory, Regret, Hope)
- Each node has different damage resistance
- Memory: Resists physical damage, weak to magic
- Regret: Resists magic damage, weak to physical
- Hope: Resists all damage, but takes bonus damage from combined attacks
- **Pass:** Player must coordinate party to damage all 3 simultaneously
- Fail condition: Any node reaches full HP (regenerates)

## Phase 3b — The Choice (Narrative)

When Elathor reaches 10% HP, the fight pauses.

*Elathor stands before the Loom's core. It is breaking.*

> **ELATHOR:** I cannot hold it much longer. The cycle will begin again. Unless...
>
> *He looks at you. For the first time, he looks... at peace.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** Unless someone takes my place. Not as a tool. As a weaver. Truly.
>
> **CHOICE A:** "I will do it." (Loom repairs, Elathor disappears, party weakened)
> **CHOICE B:** "We will do it together." (Party pools Aether, all lose something permanent)
> **CHOICE C:** "There has to be another way." (Hidden: requires 100% exploration + all collectibles)

**Consequences:**
| Choice | Elathor | Party | Ending |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| A | Sacrificed | Lose stats temporarily | Bittersweet — hero's sacrifice |
| B | Survives as mentor | Lose stats permanently | Hopeful — strength in unity |
| C (Secret) | Transcends | No loss | True ending — the new dawn |

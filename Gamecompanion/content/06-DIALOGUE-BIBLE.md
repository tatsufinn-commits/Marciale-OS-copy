# I. WRITING STYLE GUIDE

## 1.1 Voice Per Character

| Character | Register | Sentence Length | Verbal Tic | Emotional Tell |
|-----------|----------|----------------|------------|----------------|
| **Cael (Protagonist)** | Conversational, analytical | Medium | "I think..." / "Wait." | Becomes short when stressed |
| **Vaela** | Soft, hesitant | Short, trailing off | "I... never mind." | Looks away when lying |
| **Kaelen** | Blunt, direct | Short, commands | "Leave it to me." | Clenches fist when angry |
| **Sera** | Precise, scholarly | Long, qualified | "Interesting."/ "Hypothetically..." | Rambles when nervous |
| **Mira** | Warm, gentle | Medium | "Are you alright?" / "Let me help." | Oversmiles when hurting |
| **Rynn** | Abrupt, strange | Fragmentary | "..." (frequent pauses) | Changes subject when uncomfortable |
| **Orin** | Patient, knowing | Medium-long | "Ah, I remember when..." | Stops mid-sentence when remembering loss |
| **Elathor** | Ancient, weary | Measured, poetic | "I have seen..." | Long silences |

## 1.2 Key Principle: Subtext Over Exposition

Bad: *"I feel sad because my mother left me."*
Good: *"Every morning, I set an extra plate. Just in case she comes back."*

Bad: *"I'm angry that you lied to me."*
Good: *"How many times have we fought together? And you still don't trust me."*

Bad: *"The Eternal Weaver is manipulating us."*
Good: *"He helped us. But we didn't ask why."*

# II. KEY SCENES

## Scene 1: The First Meeting (Chapter 1)
*Cael encounters Vaela being bullied.*

**Setting:** Village square, Loom's Rest. Two older children are taunting a small girl with pointed ears.

> **BULLY BOY:** Look at her ears! Are you even human?
>
> **BULLY GIRL:** My mom says half-bloods bring bad luck.
>
> *Vaela doesn't respond. She's staring at the ground, arms wrapped around herself.*
>
> **CAEL (option A — Compassionate):** "Hey. Leave her alone."
>
> **CAEL (option B — Pragmatic):** *Steps between them without a word.* "She's with me."
>
> **CAEL (option C — Reckless):** "Find someone your own size to pick on. Oh wait — you already did, because you're cowards."
>
> *The bullies hesitate, then leave. Vaela looks up at Cael.*
>
> **VAELA:** You didn't have to do that.
>
> **CAEL:** Yeah, I did.
>
> **VAELA:** ...Why?
>
> *Cael pauses. They don't have a good answer.*
>
> **CAEL:** Because someone should.
>
> *Vaela's expression softens, just slightly. First bond established.*

## Scene 2: Turning Point 1 — The Riftfall (Chapter 3)
*The village is being torn apart by a Rift.*

> **Setting:** Loom's Rest during the Calamity. Screaming. Buildings dissolving into aether. The sky is wrong.

> **ORIN:** Cael! Get to the temple!
>
> **CAEL:** What about everyone else?!
>
> **ORIN:** I'll find who I can. GO!
>
> *Cael runs. The ground splits. A chasm of pure aether opens between them and the temple.*
>
> **CAEL:** Vaela! Where are you?!
>
> *No response. Debris falls. Cael shields their face.*
>
> **CAEL (internal):** *This is my fault. I should have—*
>
> *A hand grabs theirs. Vaela, bleeding from a cut on her forehead.*
>
> **VAELA:** There you are. Don't do that again.
>
> **CAEL:** Do what?
>
> **VAELA:** Disappear.
>
> *The ground gives way. They fall together into blinding white light.*
>
> **ORIN (voice, fading):** CAEL! Remember what I taught you! THE WEAVE IS— *cut off*

## Scene 3: Campfire — First Night (Chapter 4)
*The party's first night together in the wild.*

> **Setting:** Campfire in the Crystal Expanse. Four figures huddle around a small flame.

> **KAELEN:** We should reach the outpost by tomorrow. If the map's accurate.
>
> **SERA:** The map is four centuries old. It's not accurate.
>
> **KAELEN:** Then we reach something *like* an outpost by tomorrow.
>
> *Vaela laughs. It's the first time Cael has heard her laugh.*
>
> **VAELA:** I'm sorry. I just— we're all here. Alive. Together.
>
> **SERA:** That's a low bar.
>
> **VAELA:** It's a higher bar than I had this morning.
>
> *Quiet. The fire crackles.*
>
> **CAEL:** We're going to make it. All of us.
>
> *Kaelen looks at Cael. He doesn't say anything. But he nods. Just once.*

---

## Scene 4: Turning Point 3 — Orin's Death (Chapter 20)

> **Setting:** An underground chamber beneath the Fractured Coast. Orin's body is slumped against a pillar. An Echo of him flickers — his last recording.

> **ECHO OF ORIN:** Cael... if you're seeing this, I failed. I wanted to find a better way.
>
> *The Echo flickers. Damaged.*
>
> **ECHO OF ORIN:** The Eternal Weaver... he's not a god. He's a Weaver. Like you. Like me. But he's been alive so long he's forgotten what that means.
>
> *Cael kneels beside Orin's body.*
>
> **ECHO OF ORIN:** He's been using us. Every cycle. Every timeline. He finds a Weaver who can reach the Loom, and he... uses them to reset the cycle.
>
> *The Echo flickers more. Almost gone.*
>
> **ECHO OF ORIN:** But you... you're different. I don't know why. But I saw it. The Weave doesn't pull away from you. It reaches out.
>
> *Fading.*
>
> **ECHO OF ORIN:** Find the Loom. Not to reset it. To... heal it. You can do what he never could.
>
> *The Echo fades. Cael is alone with Orin's body.*
>
> **CAEL (quietly):** I'll finish it. I promise.
>
> *Party arrives. Sera gasps. Kaelen turns away. Mira begins to cry. Vaela takes Cael's hand.*

---

## Scene 5: The Eternal's Offer (Chapter 33)
*After Elathor reveals his true history.*

> **Setting:** The space between worlds. Aether threads everywhere. Elathor stands at the center, looking smaller than he did before.

> **ELATHOR:** I have woven and re-woven reality ten thousand times. Each time, I tell myself: this will be the one. This time, I will save them.
>
> *He turns to face the party.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** I have killed friends. I have let worlds burn. I have done unspeakable things because I believed the end justified the means.
>
> **CAEL:** Did it?
>
> *Long silence.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** No. It never did. But I didn't know what else to try.
>
> **VAELA:** So you used us.
>
> **ELATHOR:** Yes.
>
> **KAELEN:** And you expect us to help you?
>
> **ELATHOR:** No. I expect you to hate me. To kill me, perhaps. But you are the only hope this world has ever had. The only one who reached me.
>
> *He looks at Cael directly.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** I am offering you everything I know. And at the end... I am offering you a choice that I never had.
>
> **CAEL:** What choice?
>
> **ELATHOR:** To end the cycle. Not by resetting. By repairing. It will cost you. It may cost you everything. But it will be *real*.
>
> *He extends his hand.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** I have been the Eternal Weaver. Now... I want to be the Final Weaver. Help me end this.

---

## Scene 6: The Final Choice (Chapter 39)

> **Setting:** The Loom's core. Fractured. Bleeding aether into the void. Elathor stands before it, ready.

> **ELATHOR:** The Loom cannot be repaired by one person. It needs a sustained Aether source. I am the only one powerful enough.
>
> **CAEL:** You mean you're the only one you're willing to sacrifice.
>
> *Elathor smiles. It's sad.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** ...You see through me. Even now.
>
> **CAEL:** I learned from the best manipulator in existence.
>
> *A beat. Then Elathor laughs. It's the first genuine laugh the party has ever heard from him.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** Good. Good. You *are* ready.
>
> *He turns to the Loom.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** I could pour myself into it. Every cycle, every memory, every thread of my existence. It would hold. For another thousand years. Until the next Weaver is needed.
>
> *He looks back.*
>
> **ELATHOR:** Or you could try something else. Something I never dared.
>
> **CAEL:** What?
>
> **ELATHOR:** All of you. Together. Pour your strength into the Loom. Not enough to destroy you, but enough to change you. You would carry the Weave's burden. Not me.
>
> *The party exchanges glances.*
>
> **MIRA:** What would it cost us?
>
> **ELATHOR:** Something permanent. A piece of your Aether. You will be weaker. But you will be *free*.
>
> **CAEL:** And you?
>
> **ELATHOR:** I would... rest. For the first time in eternity.
>
> *Choice appears:*
>
> **A:** "I'll do it. Alone." (Cael sacrifices stats, Elathor fades)
> **B:** "We'll do it together." (Party loses permanent stats, Elathor survives as mentor)
> **C (Hidden):** "There's another way." (Requires 100% secrets found)

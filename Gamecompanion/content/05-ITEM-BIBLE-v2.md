## 1.2 Magic Ranks (7 Tiers)

| Rank | Description | Gameplay Mechanic |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Beginner** | Basic spells (Stone Bullet, Water Ball) | Low mana cost, low damage |
| **Intermediate** | Stronger spells (Fireball, Wind Cutter) | Moderate cost, AoE potential |
| **Advanced** | Powerful spells | High damage, significant mana |
| **Saint** | Area-of-effect devastation | Very high damage, long cooldown |
| **King** | Army-level destruction | Massive damage, strategic use |
| **Emperor** | Kingdom-level power | End-game only |
| **God** | Continental-scale | Lore-only, not player-accessible |
